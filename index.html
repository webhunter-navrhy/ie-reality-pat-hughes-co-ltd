About the property

Description property

The property comprises a mixed-use property which provides a detached two storey period residence, detached single storey former filling station/ retail unit and a detached double bay garage.

Internally the residence is laid out to provide a sitting room, kitchen cum dining room and utility room at ground floor level, and 4 no. bedrooms with family bathroom at first floor level.

The commercial unit is laid out to provide small retail area with side office and stores to the rear of the unit.

The property also has the benefit of a secure double-sided garage on the northern side of the residence.

The property may qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant.

The property may be sold in one or two lots subject to interest.
